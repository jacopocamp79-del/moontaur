THE MIND BEHIND THE FOREST

Monray Roe

Poet · Songwriter · Creator of MOONTAUR

♛　✦　♛

I was never meant to fit in.

“I always knew I was never quite like the others. I've spent my life feeling out of place, as if I belonged to a world I had yet to create.”

My name is Monray Roe. I come from Ascoli Piceno, Italy, and I have always been drawn to languages, stories, music and the ways people express who they are. I am a poet and singer-songwriter: I write songs and poems to give shape to things that can be difficult to explain, such as nostalgia, identity, longing, transformation, beauty, anger and tenderness.

Today, I live in the Netherlands, where I study Media and Culture at Utrecht University. My studies allow me to explore the relationship between media, storytelling, art and culture, while I continue developing my own creative voice as Monray Roe.

Alongside my studies and my writing, I am creating MOONTAUR — a personal universe that brings together poetry, music, reflection, symbolism and imagination. I want it to feel like a forest to enter when the outside world becomes too loud: a kingdom built from words, memories and dreams, and a place where ideas can become something real.

I choose it. I become it. I make it real.

This is not a finished portrait. It is a world still being written.
